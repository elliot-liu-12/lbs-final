## Verdicts against ground truth

| checker | leaky & rejected (TP) | safe & accepted (TN) | safe but rejected (conservative, FP) | leaky but accepted (FN) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| CT mode | 12 | 13 | 5 | 0 |
| NI mode (baseline) | 5 | 16 | 2 | 7 |

- 30/30 examples match their expected verdict and diagnostic codes in both modes.
- Conservative CT rejections (5): cons_constant_bound, cons_dead_guard, cons_flow_insensitive, cons_times_zero, cons_xor_self_index.
- Leaks the NI baseline misses (7): leak_balanced_branch, leak_early_exit, leak_histogram, leak_magic_value, leak_modexp_branch, leak_sbox_lookup, leak_secret_length — all are timing/address leaks that do not affect the final public state.
- Random two-run testing (500 trials) witnessed 11/12 leaky examples and never reported a divergence for an accepted program.
- Needed hand-picked inputs to witness: leak_magic_value — a leak the type checker catches but random testing is very unlikely to hit.
- Total CT checking time for all 30 examples: 0.11 ms (median per example 0.003 ms; parsing excluded).
